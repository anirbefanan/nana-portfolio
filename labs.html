ORION

A rule-based market decision assistant for independent trading operations. Designed to reduce continuous manual monitoring and inconsistent execution, and improve visibility, repeatability, and risk-control discipline.

OSystem 01
Risk screening & triage

KYB Sentinel

For KYB and risk reviewers. A screening and triage tool addressing manual effort and inconsistent reviews.

Modeled potential: ~30–50% less first-pass manual screening effort; improved consistency and traceability.

Payments operations

MDR Bulk Adjustment Tool

For payments and backend operations. A bulk-change preparation tool addressing manual file work and mapping risk.

Modeled potential: ~40–60% less preparation effort; lower mapping and rework risk.

Workforce planning

Workload & Manpower Allocation Model

For operations leaders and workforce planners. A scenario model addressing ad-hoc allocation and capacity trade-offs.

Modeled potential: ~10–20% better planning speed and capacity utilization.

Forecasting & headcount

Merchant Funnel Forecast & Headcount Model

For operations planning teams. A forecast model addressing recurring preparation effort and late visibility of staffing gaps.

Modeled potential: ~30–50% less forecast-preparation effort; earlier staffing-gap visibility.

SOP, Governance & Findings Toolkit

SOP / Governance Toolkit

For operations, quality, and governance teams. Reusable standards addressing inconsistent interpretation and unclear ownership.

Can reduce interpretation effort and improve consistency and audit readiness. The CV models ~20–30% less interpretation/reporting effort for the combined toolkit.

SOP, Governance & Findings Toolkit

Project Updates / Findings Memo

For project teams and leaders. Structured updates addressing scattered findings, unclear ownership, and reporting effort.

Can improve accountability and management visibility. Part of the same combined toolkit estimate, not a separate measured outcome.
