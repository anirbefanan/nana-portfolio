Workload & Productivity Optimization

Problem
Demand, effort, and capacity lack one planning view.
My role
Model workload and build decision support.
Solution

Assumptions 48 team-hours/day; 20% lost; half recoverable.

Calculation 48 × 20% × 50% = 4.8 hours recovered.
