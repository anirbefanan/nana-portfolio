07 / Direction

What failed.
What stayed.

Progress came from removing complexity as often as adding capability.

Did not work

  • Over-complex decision variants
  • Unstable risk-control experiments
  • Excessive constraints that blocked valid actions
  • Time-dependent controls that weakened outcomes
  • Production defects that interrupted execution

Final direction

  • One clear decision framework
  • Risk-controlled exit logic
  • Consistent risk controls
  • Validated-data decisions
  • Simple mobile operations
  • Separate production fixes and research
  • Automated checks before deployment

10 / Lessons

What the build
made clear.

The strongest lessons extend beyond trading systems.

01

Simple systems are easier to trust and operate.

02

Decision-model changes must stay separate from production fixes.

03

Research alignment matters more than adding more features.

04

Risk control is part of the operating system, not an add-on.

05

Operational reliability matters as much as decision quality.

06

Automation should reduce decision errors—not only save time.
